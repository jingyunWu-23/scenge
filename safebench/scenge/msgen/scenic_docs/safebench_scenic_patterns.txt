SafeBench Scenic pattern notes.

Typical file structure:

model scenic.simulators.carla.model

Town = globalParameters.town
EgoSpawnPt = OrientedPoint at globalParameters.spawnPt,
    with heading globalParameters.yaw deg

param map = localPath(f'../maps/{Town}.xodr')
param carla_map = Town
param OPT_GEO_X_DISTANCE = Range(...)
param OPT_GEO_Y_DISTANCE = Range(...)

ego = Car at EgoSpawnPt,
    with regionContainedIn None,
    with blueprint EGO_MODEL

Adversarial actors should be Cars and should have behavior names containing Adv so SafeBench can identify them. Prefer positions relative to an OrientedPoint following roadDirection from EgoSpawnPt. For adjacent-lane placement, use `left of` or `right of` an oriented point by a tunable lateral distance. For same-lane leading vehicles, use an OrientedPoint following roadDirection from EgoSpawnPt by a tunable longitudinal distance.

Behaviors should be simple and robust: drive forward at a target speed, wait until ego is close, brake, or perform a lane insertion. Keep generated scripts conservative and compatible with Scenic 2.x.
